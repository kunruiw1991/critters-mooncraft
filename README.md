# CritterCraft: Moonless Night 🌑🚀🧱

A 3D Minecraft-style Voxel Strategy & Base-Building game starring **CatNap**, **LunaBat (Chuyu)**, **SunnyFox (Mom)**, **PoppyDash (Dad)**, **Mikey & JJ**, and the **Smiling Critters**!

**Play Live:** https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-mooncraft/

## 🎬 10-Second Opening Cinematics (月黑风高)
1. **0.0s – 3.2s (Cute CatNap → Nightmare CatNap):** On a dark, windy night (`月黑风高`), Cute CatNap stands under the glowing 3D Voxel Full Moon. Swirling purple dream-mist transforms him into towering **Nightmare CatNap**!
2. **3.2s – 6.8s (Rocket to the Moon → Moon Vanishes):** Nightmare CatNap launches a Red-Smoke Voxel Rocket into the sky, shattering the Full Moon into **100 Moon Shards** (`the moon is gone`)!
3. **6.8s – 10.0s (Zombies Emerge & Smiling Critters Rally):** Blocky Minecraft Zombies emerge from the ground while the Smiling Critters hop in around the Moon Altar to build, defend, and restore the Moon!

## 🐾 10 Critter Units Across 3 Functional Roles
- **⛏️ PRODUCE (Economy, Light & Buffs):**
  - **SunnyFox — Lantern Sun-Forge (`25 🌟`):** Generates `+9 🌟/4s` and casts a **3.4-tile Lantern Aura** that removes Moonless Night Fog and slows zombies.
  - **PoppyDash — Popcorn Windmill (`35 🌟`):** Generates `+11 🌟/4.2s` and boosts nearby critters' attack/production speed by **+35%**.
  - **PickyPiggy — Berry Heal Orchard (`30 🌟`):** Generates `+7 🌟/4s` and heals adjacent friendly critters and walls (`+14 HP/s`).
- **🛡️ DEFEND (Voxel Walls, Moats & High-Ground Watchtowers):**
  - **Bobby BearHug — Obsidian Hug-Wall (`20 🌟`):** `680 HP` fortress block that attracts zombies and reflects `10` cuddle-damage.
  - **Bubba Bubbaphant — Bubble Moat Dome (`30 🌟`):** Slows zombies in a `2.8-tile` water moat (`-52% speed`) and shields nearby allies (`-25% damage taken`).
  - **Mikey & JJ — Cobblestone Watchtower + TNT (`25 🌟`):** Stack ANY Attack Critter **ON TOP** for **+40% range & +25% damage**! Pops a `95 AoE` mini-TNT if broken.
- **⚔️ ATTACK (Pierce, Splash, Beam & Chain):**
  - **LunaBat — Moon-Star Crossbow (`40 🌟`):** Pierces `2` zombies in a line and harvests `+1` bonus Moon Shard per zombie.
  - **DogDay — Solar Splash Cannon (`55 🌟`):** Fires `1.65-tile` AoE sunburst splash orbs.
  - **CraftyCorn — Rainbow Prism Beam (`50 🌟`):** `5.8-tile` sniper beam that paints zombies so they take **+30% damage** from all critters.
  - **KickinChicken & Hoppy — Thunder Bolt Striker (`45 🌟`):** Chains lightning across `3` zombies and knocks front-line zombies backward.

## ⚖️ 5-Pillar Balance State System
1. **Moonless Night Fog & Role Triangle:** Unlit tiles outside `SunnyFox` Lanterns suffer `-30%` range, making Produce + Defend + Attack interdependent.
2. **Anti-Spam Dynamic Cost (`baseCost × 1.15^count`):** Encourages diverse critter villages instead of spamming one unit.
3. **Critter Harmony Synergy (`Lv.1 +15%` / `Lv.2 +30%` / `Lv.3 +45%`):** Activated by combining Produce, Defend, and Attack roles.
4. **Counter-Archetype Zombies:** Blockhead Walkers, Baby Runners, Iron-Bucket Tanks, Pickaxe Miners, and Nightmare Smoke Goliaths.
5. **Adaptive Flow Director (`P = ZombieThreat / (DefensePower + 5.5 × EconomyRate)`):** Keeps gameplay inside the `[0.75, 1.15]` sweet spot with automatic SunnyFox Care Packages for 5-year-old accessibility.
